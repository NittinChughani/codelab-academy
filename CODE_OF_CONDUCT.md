# Code of Conduct

Our community aims to be welcoming, respectful, and supportive of learners of all backgrounds and skill levels.

Be kind, give constructive feedback, assume good faith, and respect differing perspectives. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable.

If you encounter a problem, contact the repository maintainers privately using an appropriate contact method they have publicly provided. Maintainers may remove harmful contributions or restrict participation to keep the community safe.
